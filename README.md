# 🧸 KiddosCare

> A full-stack pediatric healthcare platform designed to simplify child healthcare management through digital patient records, vaccination tracking, appointment management, and automated communication.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [kiddoscare.in](https://kiddoscare.in?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## 📌 Overview

**KiddosCare** is a web-based pediatric healthcare management platform that helps healthcare providers and parents efficiently manage child healthcare information.

The platform provides a centralized system for maintaining patient records, managing appointments, tracking vaccination schedules, and sending important notifications through WhatsApp.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👶 Patient Management

* Register and manage pediatric patient information
* Maintain child and parent details
* Access centralized patient records
* Update and manage patient profiles

### 💉 Vaccination Management

* Track vaccination schedules
* Monitor upcoming and completed vaccines
* Maintain vaccination history
* Update vaccination status

### 📅 Appointment Management

* Schedule and manage patient appointments
* Maintain appointment records
* View upcoming consultations

### 🔔 Automated Notifications

* WhatsApp notifications for important updates
* Vaccination reminders
* Patient registration notifications
* Vaccination completion notifications

### 👨‍⚕️ Healthcare Dashboard

* Centralized dashboard for healthcare professionals
* Quick access to patient information
* Efficient management of pediatric healthcare workflows
